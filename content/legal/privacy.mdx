## 1. Who we are

MedAI Academy ("we", "us") provides online courses for healthcare professionals. Contact: hello@medaiacademy.com.

## 2. Information we collect

- **Account information:** your name, email address, profession and organisation.
- **Learning information:** courses you enrol in, lessons completed, and certificates issued.
- **Payment information:** payment method, transaction ID, amount and the payment screenshot you upload. We do not receive or store your bank card or wallet PIN.
- **Messages:** information you send through our contact or institution enquiry forms.
- **Usage information:** anonymous, aggregated visitor statistics (for example pages viewed) collected through privacy-friendly analytics.

## 3. How we use your information

- To create and manage your account and give you access to courses
- To verify payments and send enrolment confirmations
- To track your progress and issue certificates
- To reply to your messages and enquiries
- To send our newsletter, if you subscribed (you can unsubscribe at any time)
- To improve our website and courses

## 4. Legal basis

We process your information to provide the service you requested (our contract with you), to meet legal obligations, and — for the newsletter — with your consent.

## 5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the website, such as our hosting, database, authentication and email providers, under appropriate agreements. Certificate verification pages show your name, course title and completion date to anyone who has the certificate ID.

## 6. Data retention

We keep your account and learning records while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards so certificates can be verified. Payment records are kept as required for accounting purposes.

## 7. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to certain processing. Contact us at hello@medaiacademy.com.

## 8. Security

We use reputable providers, encryption in transit, and access controls so that users can only see their own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

## 9. Patient information

Do not send us any patient information through our forms, uploads or messages.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date above shows the latest version.
