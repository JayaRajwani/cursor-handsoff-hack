## Our promise

We want you to be confident before you buy, which is why the first lessons of every course are free to preview.

## Eligibility

You may request a full refund within **7 days** of your enrolment being approved, provided you have completed **no more than 25%** of the course lessons and have not downloaded a certificate.

## How to request a refund

Email hello@medaiacademy.com from the email address on your account with the course name and your transaction ID. We aim to respond within three working days.

## How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method (bank account, JazzCash or Easypaisa) within 10 working days. We are not responsible for fees charged by your bank or wallet provider.

## Duplicate or incorrect payments

If you paid twice or paid the wrong amount, contact us and we will correct or refund the difference.

## Institutional purchases

Group and institutional purchases are governed by the agreement or invoice terms agreed with your organisation.
