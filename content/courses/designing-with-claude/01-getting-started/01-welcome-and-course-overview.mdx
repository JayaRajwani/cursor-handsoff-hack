Welcome to **Designing with Claude for Healthcare Professionals**. Whether you are a consultant preparing a grand-round talk, a nurse educator updating ward induction material, a pharmacist writing a medication leaflet or a student designing a conference poster, this course will help you produce clearer, more professional work in less time — safely.

## Why this course exists

Healthcare professionals spend a surprising amount of time on communication work: leaflets, posters, slide decks, teaching sessions, newsletters and training packs. This work matters. A well-written leaflet improves adherence; a clear induction pack reduces errors; a good presentation changes practice.

Yet most of us were never taught design or plain-language writing, and we rarely have time for it. AI assistants such as Claude can help you draft, structure, simplify and polish this material quickly. Used carelessly, though, they can also introduce errors, outdated advice or privacy breaches. This course teaches you to get the benefits while managing the risks.

<Callout type="note" title="What this course is — and is not">

This is a course about **communication and education materials**. It is not about using AI for diagnosis or treatment decisions. Everything you create with AI remains your professional responsibility and must be reviewed by a qualified person before use.

</Callout>

## How the course is organised

The course has four modules. Each lesson takes 10–25 minutes and ends with a short practical exercise.

1. **Getting started with Claude** — what Claude is, how to set it up, and the safety rules that apply to everything else.
2. **Writing effective prompts** — a simple framework (we call it **CLEAR**) for prompts that produce accurate, well-structured output.
3. **Designing patient education materials** — leaflets, posters and infographics that patients can actually understand.
4. **Presentations and staff training** — slide decks, teaching sessions, case scenarios and quizzes, plus a workflow your team can trust.

## How to get the most out of it

- **Have Claude open in another tab** while you learn. You will learn far more by trying each prompt than by reading about it.
- **Use invented or anonymised examples only.** Throughout the course we use fictional patients and scenarios. Please do the same.
- **Work on a real project.** Pick one thing you actually need — a leaflet for your clinic, a talk you are giving next month — and build it as you go.
- **Mark each lesson complete** to track your progress. When you finish every lesson you can download your certificate.

<Exercise title="Before you start">

Write down one piece of communication or teaching material you would like to create or improve in the next month. Keep it in mind — you will build it step by step during this course.

</Exercise>
