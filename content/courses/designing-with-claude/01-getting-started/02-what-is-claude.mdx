Claude is an AI assistant made by **Anthropic**, an AI safety and research company. You talk to Claude in ordinary language — by typing or uploading documents — and it responds with writing, explanations, summaries, tables, code and even interactive documents. You can use Claude in a web browser at claude.ai, in desktop apps, or in mobile apps for iPhone and Android.

## A plain-language explanation of how it works

Claude is a **large language model** (LLM). It was trained on a very large amount of text so that it learned the patterns of language: how ideas are explained, how documents are structured, how arguments are made. When you write a request, Claude predicts — word by word — a useful response based on those patterns and on the instructions and information you give it.

Three consequences matter for healthcare work:

1. **Claude is excellent with language and structure.** Drafting, rewriting for a different audience, summarising, organising and formatting are its strengths.
2. **Claude does not "look things up" by default.** Unless you give it a source document or use a feature that searches the web, it answers from patterns it learned during training, which have a cut-off date and may be out of date.
3. **Claude can be confidently wrong.** It can produce statements, numbers or references that sound plausible but are inaccurate. This is often called *hallucination*. It is why every clinical fact must be checked.

<Callout type="warning" title="Treat Claude like a very fast, well-read assistant — not an authority">

Claude can give you an excellent first draft in seconds. But just as you would check a draft written by a new junior colleague, you must check Claude's work against trusted sources such as national guidelines, your formulary and local protocols.

</Callout>

## What Claude is good at in healthcare communication

- Turning technical text into **plain language** for patients and families
- Producing **structured drafts**: leaflets, posters, slide outlines, lesson plans, checklists
- **Rewriting** content for different audiences — patients, nurses, consultants, managers
- **Summarising** long documents such as guidelines or policies you upload
- Generating **case scenarios, quiz questions and role-play scripts** for teaching
- Suggesting **layouts, headings and visual ideas**, and building simple visual designs

## What it is not good at (or not suitable for)

- Making or confirming clinical decisions, doses or diagnoses
- Providing up-to-date guideline recommendations without a source document
- Producing reliable references — always verify every citation
- Handling any identifiable patient information (see the next lesson)

## Useful features to know about

Claude's features and plan names change regularly, so check claude.ai for the current details. The features most useful for this course are:

- **Chat** — the main conversation window where you write prompts.
- **File uploads** — you can attach documents (for example a PDF guideline or a draft leaflet) and ask Claude to work with them.
- **Artifacts** — for longer pieces of work such as a document, a web page, a diagram or a simple interactive tool, Claude can show the result in a separate panel beside the chat, where you can view, copy and download it. This is very useful for posters and infographics.
- **Projects** — on some plans you can group chats and reference documents together, for example a "Ward induction" project containing your unit's policies and style guide.
- **File creation** — depending on your plan and settings, Claude can create downloadable files such as Word documents, spreadsheets, PDFs and slide presentations.

## Setting up your account

1. Go to **claude.ai** and sign up with your email address (a free plan is available).
2. Open **Settings** and review the **privacy settings**, including whether your conversations may be used to improve Anthropic's models. Choose the option that matches your institution's policy.
3. If your organisation provides an approved business or enterprise account, use that for work tasks instead of a personal account.
4. Bookmark claude.ai or install the app on your phone.

<Callout type="privacy" title="Personal versus organisational accounts">

Consumer accounts and organisational (team or enterprise) accounts come with different terms about data handling. Ask your information governance or IT team which AI tools, if any, are approved for work use. Regardless of the account type, never enter identifiable patient information.

</Callout>

<KeyTakeaways>

- Claude is a large language model: brilliant with language and structure, but it can be confidently wrong.
- Use it to draft, simplify, structure and design — never as the final authority on clinical facts.
- Check your privacy settings and your institution's policy before using Claude for work.
