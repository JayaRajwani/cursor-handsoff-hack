# AI Safety Checklist for Healthcare Materials

MedAI Academy — Designing with Claude for Healthcare Professionals
(Print this page and keep it beside your computer.)

## Before you start

- [ ] I am using an AI tool approved (or permitted) by my organisation for this task
- [ ] I have checked the tool's privacy settings
- [ ] My prompt and any uploaded files contain NO identifiable patient information
      (names, MR/ID/CNIC numbers, dates of birth, addresses, photos, rare-case details)
- [ ] My prompt contains no identifiable staff information or confidential business data
- [ ] I have a trusted, current, non-confidential source to base the content on

## While drafting

- [ ] I used the CLEAR framework (Context, Listener, Expected output, Accuracy guardrails, Review)
- [ ] I asked the AI to flag clinical claims that need verification
- [ ] I kept my own master copy of the document outside the chat

## Before anyone else sees it

- [ ] Every clinical fact (doses, thresholds, timings, statistics) checked against the source
- [ ] Every reference confirmed to exist and to support the statement
- [ ] Nothing important has been lost when simplifying (caveats, warnings, contraindications)
- [ ] Reading level appropriate for the audience; medical terms explained
- [ ] Culturally appropriate, inclusive and respectful language
- [ ] Accessible: font size 12pt+, high contrast, colour not used alone to convey meaning
- [ ] Images are owned, licensed or created for us; no patient photos without consent
- [ ] Reviewed by a qualified clinician
- [ ] Tested with a lay reader / patient representative (patient materials)

## Approval and records

- [ ] Approved through my organisation's normal process
- [ ] Version number, publication date, review date and owner added
- [ ] AI assistance recorded where policy requires

Educational resource only. Always follow your institution's policies and local regulations.
