## The seven-step safe workflow

1. **Define** — audience, purpose, key actions, format and source.
2. **Protect** — confirm no identifiable patient or staff information will be used; use an approved tool.
3. **Prompt** — use the CLEAR framework and upload your non-confidential source.
4. **Refine** — iterate with specific feedback; keep your own master document.
5. **Verify** — clinical accuracy, readability, inclusion and accessibility.
6. **Approve** — through your organisation's normal process, with version and review dates.
7. **Record** — note the AI tool used, author, reviewers and dates.

<Callout type="note" title="Make it visible">

Print the workflow and the AI safety checklist and display them where your team creates materials. Consistency is what turns individual skill into organisational safety.

</Callout>

## Introducing AI to your team or department

If you want to help colleagues adopt AI safely:

- **Start small** with low-risk tasks such as formatting, internal training material or meeting summaries.
- **Talk to information governance early.** Ask which tools are approved and what the rules are.
- **Share examples** — before-and-after leaflets are persuasive.
- **Share your prompt library** and review it together.
- **Agree on review responsibilities** so AI-assisted material is never published unchecked.

## Keeping your skills current

AI tools change quickly: new features, new models and new terms of use appear regularly. To stay current:

- Revisit your tool's help pages and privacy settings every few months.
- Follow your professional body's and regulator's guidance on AI use.
- Read our blog for practical updates written for healthcare professionals.
- Keep practising: the more you use structured prompts, the better your results.
